📖 How to play

1. 👶 Choose a baby: Tap Mia, Noah, Lily or Oliver in the Daycare tab to care for them.

2. 🍼 Meet their needs: Feed, play, clean, cuddle and give them naps. Your babies' needs change as the day goes on.

3. 🪙 Earn coins: Every care activity gives you coins and XP. Level up to earn bonus coins.

4. 🛍️ Visit the shops: Buy snacks, reusable toys, room decorations and outfits. Food is consumed when used; toys and outfits can be reused.

5. 🎒 Use your inventory: Select an item and tap Use, Place or Wear. Decorations can be turned on and off. Select a baby before using food, toys or clothes.

6. 🎯 Complete missions: Claim prizes when you finish each mission.

7. 💾 Save automatically: Your progress is stored on this device in your browser.
